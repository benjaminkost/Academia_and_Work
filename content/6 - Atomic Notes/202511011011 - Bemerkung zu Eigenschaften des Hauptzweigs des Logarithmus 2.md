$$ \exp(\text{Log}(z)) = z \quad \text{für alle } z \in \mathbb{C}^\times $$
$$ \text{Log}(\exp(w)) = w \quad \text{für alle } w \in \mathbb{R} + i(-\pi, \pi]. $$
